How to enter a melody
  • Mouse: click the grid to add a note, drag to set its length. Drag a note to move it, drag its right edge to resize it. Right-click a note (or select it and press Delete) to remove it.
  • Computer keyboard: keys A–; are the white keys and W E T Y U O P the black keys. Notes are added at the cursor (the yellow line; click the bar numbers to move it). 1–5 pick the length, . dotted, Space rest, Backspace delete, Z/X octave, ←/→ move the cursor.
  • MIDI keyboard: press Connect, then play notes. They are added at the cursor like typed notes.
